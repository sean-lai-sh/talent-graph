Talent Graph · V2 · Exploratory

The network cannot know who is good at spotting people until time has passed.

Every referral is a prediction. After a 180-day observation window the graph scores that prediction against what the person actually did — never against other judges — and quietly turns the error into a reliability weight. Play the next seven months of the seed.

Why we cannot just ask the herd

If we weighted judges by how much they agree with each other, we would reward the clique and punish the person who named someone early. Agreement is cheap. Outcomes are not. Until outcomes exist, every judge is weighted exactly 1 — V2 reproduces V0 bit for bit. That is the point.

        The update

        1. x = Ruv — the referral’s own strength. This is the prediction. It is never rewritten by the weight that follows.
        2. truth — residual outcome percentile of the candidate after the window, opportunity-corrected. Not a pairwise estimate.
        3. E = (x − truth)² then Ē ← (1−η)Ē + ηE in the order predictions become eligible. η = 0.3
        4. p = exp(−τ Ē), p̂ = n/(n+λ)·p + λ/(n+λ)·1. τ = 4, λ = 3. New judges stay near 1.
        5. The Referral Signal uses p̂ · Ruv. Zero-weight judges drop out of the Top-K so they cannot dilute it.
